# SAHARA — Design Decision Summary

**Phase 4.** The key visual decisions, in one place. Full detail across the other `/design/` files.

## Final visual direction
**"Rooted & Warm"** — a grounded, human, credible NGO look built on the **banyan-tree** metaphor (*sahara* = support/shelter). Warm cream canvas, authentic documentary photography, editorial serif + clean sans, one warm accent for action. Deliberately **not** corporate-SaaS, not childish, minimal gradients/animation.

## Colors (derived from the actual logo)
- **Brand green #018738** (sampled from logo) · **Forest green #0B4D2A** (headings/nav/footer/text) · green tints #E9F3EC / #D3E7D8.
- **Terracotta #C24A25** = primary CTA/Donate · **Marigold #F2A61C** = accents/focus.
- **Cream #FBF8F1** page · **Paper #FFF** cards · **Ink #1B1A17** text · **#5A5750** muted · **#E7E1D5** lines.
- Green **duotone** for historical/archive images.

## Typography
- **Fraunces** (warm serif) for headings/display; **Inter** for body/UI (tabular figures); **Noto Telugu** if bilingual [Verify]. Fluid mobile-first scale; body ≥16px.

## Hero treatment
Full-bleed **P01** (girls with school bags) + forest scrim; "SINCE 1986" eyebrow; Fraunces H1; Donate + "See our work"; 16:9 desktop / 4:5 face-safe mobile; optional slow zoom (reduced-motion off).

## Program treatment
Six equal image-led cards with line-icon chips; honest status labels; Health uses a shared photo (no fabricated image); contextual giving CTAs.

## Timeline treatment
Signature **banyan-branch** vertical timeline; forest spine + leaf nodes; year in Fraunces/terracotta; source chips; historical images duotone; real photos where they fit, icons otherwise (never fabricated); expand-on-tap; reduced-motion safe. Founding shown as **1986** per client; infographic JPEGs not used as backgrounds.

## Gallery treatment
Curated masonry with program-category filter chips; accessible lightbox (keyboard + swipe); factual captions; lazy-loaded; historical tiles duotone. Not a flat dump.

## Donation treatment
Clean **account cards** for **all client-approved accounts** — 5 Indian (Vijaya **404601010006102**, Canara, Bank of Baroda **BARB0BARKAT**, ICICI, Union Bank) + 2 Foreign (Canara SWIFT CNRBINBBHFD, SBI SWIFT SBININBB104) + UPI (PhonePe 9440483089 / GPay 9441156212) + **Cash** + approved platforms (Give India, ImpactGuru, Milaap). **One-tap copy** on every number. **No FCRA anywhere.** Figures never altered; Union IFSC flagged "confirm before transferring."

## Footer treatment
Forest-green, cream text, 5 columns, tagline **"HELP POOR AND INDEED PEOPLE,"** socials **FB/IG/X/LinkedIn** (client-confirmed), confirmed contact, Registered Society/80G/12A (**no FCRA**), banyan motif.

## Mobile strategy
Mobile-first, native-feeling: hamburger→forest drawer, **sticky Donate bar**, face-safe hero crops, bank details as **cards not tables**, single-column forms, tap-to-call/copy, strict image optimization for low bandwidth.

## Most important visual decisions
1. Palette **sampled from the real logo** (#018738) — authentic, not invented.
2. **Warm terracotta** as the single action accent so **Donate always stands out**.
3. **Authentic documentary photos** over stock; consistent warm grade + duotone for history.
4. **Timeline as the hero storytelling feature** (banyan branch).
5. **No impact counters** (client gave no current stats) — credibility via trust band, reports, "board unpaid."
6. **No FCRA** anywhere; **all approved donation accounts** shown with copy-to-clipboard.
7. Accessibility & performance first (AA contrast using forest-green for text, mobile-first, lazy images).

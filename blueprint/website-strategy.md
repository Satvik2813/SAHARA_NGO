## 1. Primary purpose of the website

**Turn a 35+ year, self-documented grassroots track record into online trust and action** — so a first-time visitor quickly understands *who SAHARA is, that it is real and credible, the good it has done, and how to help* — and can take a confident next step (donate / sponsor / partner / volunteer / contact).

Supporting purposes:
- Present a **modern, warm, trustworthy** organization that happens to have a **long history** (not a redesigned 1990s site).
- Consolidate scattered, inconsistent, sometimes out-of-date information (three sources) into **one authoritative, verified** presence.
- Provide **transparency** (annual reports, registration, governance) that de-risks giving.
- Preserve **authenticity and history** while looking contemporary.

## 2. Primary audiences (UX assumptions, not factual claims)

| Audience | What they need | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual donors** (Indian, incl. diaspora) | Proof of legitimacy + a clear, specific way to give | Donate / Sponsor a child / Adopt an elder |
| **First-time discoverers** | "What is this, is it real, what do they do?" in <30s | Understand → Donate/Explore |
| **Potential partners / funders / CSR** | Track record, governance, reports, scale, focus areas | Partner / Download reports / Contact |

## 3. Secondary audiences

| Audience | What they need | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Volunteers / interns / students** | How to help with time/skills; intern history | Volunteer / Contact |
| **Journalists** | Facts, history, press archive, spokesperson/contact | Press page / Contact |
| **History researchers / alumni beneficiaries** | The org's journey and milestones | Timeline / Stories |
| **Verifiers / due-diligence** | Registration, 80G/12A/FCRA, reports, address | Transparency / Contact |
| **Existing beneficiaries / community** | Programs, training, contact, schedule | Programs / Contact |

## 4. Main user actions (in priority order)

1. **Donate** (primary conversion) — but **blocked on verified payment details** (`[Verify]`, conflicts §D).
2. **Sponsor a child / Adopt an elder** (specific, emotive giving).
3. **Explore programs / impact / stories** (build conviction).
4. **Verify legitimacy** (reports, registration, governance).
5. **Get involved** (volunteer / intern / partner).
6. **Contact** (enquire, visit, media).

## 5. Trust-building objectives

- Lead with **evidence**: 35+ years, dated milestones, six program areas, real photos of real work.
- Surface **credentials**: Registered Society, 80G, 12A — each **[Verify currency]**. **NO FCRA** on the site (client instruction) — do not display FCRA number/status/badge.
- Publish **annual reports (2013–2018 available; request newer)** and **"zero board remuneration"** governance signal [WP].
- Show **named partners/funders** (CIF, DDS, Give India, Rotary, UNICEF, Sight & Life, Andheri Hilfe, Sitaram Jindal…) — active vs past to be confirmed.
- Consistent, **verified** contact + a real address in Amberpet, Hyderabad.

## 6. Emotional objectives

- **Warmth + dignity, not pity.** Show agency and outcomes (a trained tailor earning; a child in school; an elder cared for *within* the community, not warehoused).
- **Hope and continuity** — "extending a helping hand" across generations (the beneficiary-turned-donor arc).
- **Quiet credibility** — calm confidence of an organization that has quietly done this for decades.
- Avoid: poverty-voyeurism, childish/cartoonish styling, corporate-startup gloss.

## 7. Information architecture principles

1. **Evidence-first storytelling** — every claim is shown, then optionally proven (report/story/photo).
2. **One fact, one source of truth** — reconcile the three sources into single verified statements; keep `[Verify]` where unresolved.
3. **Separate *current* from *historical*** — programs, stats, registration and contacts are labeled by time/status; never blend cumulative-historical with current numbers.
4. **Donation is always one tap away** — persistent, but honest (no unverified bank details).
5. **Shallow, scannable, mobile-first** — most journeys ≤2 clicks; long pages with anchored sections over deep trees.
6. **Progressive depth** — homepage teases; dedicated pages prove; reports/timeline satisfy researchers.
7. **Accessibility & performance as first-class** (image-heavy site in a low-bandwidth region).
8. **Preserve authenticity** — real SAHARA photos and history over stock; rebuild (don't embed) the old timeline/graphics natively.

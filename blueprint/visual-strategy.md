# SAHARA — Photo & Visual Strategy

**Phase 2 — planning only. Do NOT extract or edit any image yet** (that is the next phase, with Adobe/Canva). This defines *what* imagery is needed and *which* audited assets can supply it.

Basis: `asset-inventory.md`. Legend: **[Client] [WP] [Verify]**. Quality note: most SAHARA photos are **low-to-medium resolution (web/WhatsApp-grade, ~2012–2022)**; many have banner/bank text baked in and need cropping; several need upscaling/restoration.

---

## Guiding principles
- **Authenticity first** — real SAHARA photos over stock; they *are* the credibility. Reserve stock only for abstract backgrounds.
- **Dignity** — agency and warmth, not pity; consent required for identifiable beneficiaries (esp. minors) [Verify].
- **Consistency** — a unified treatment (see `design-directions.md`) will harmonize mismatched source quality.
- **Performance** — image-heavy site in a low-bandwidth region → strict optimization (`performance.md`).

## Category-by-category needs

### 1. Hero images
- **Need:** 1 primary + 2–4 rotating/section heroes.
- **Orientation/ratio:** landscape 16:9 (desktop) with a **portrait-safe 4:5 crop** for mobile.
- **Quality:** high; **≥1600px wide** preferred → most candidates need **upscaling**.
- **Candidates [Client]:** `41 (2).JPG` (girls w/ school bags, 929×557), `tailor grop.JPG` (women sewing, 1012×524), `children.JPG` (children, hands raised, 594×405).
- **Existing sufficient?** Marginal — usable after **crop + upscale + color grade**. Consider requesting **high-res originals** from client.
- **Extraction:** No (standalone). **Adobe/Canva:** Yes (upscale, grade, overlay-safe crop).

### 2. Program images (6)
- **Need:** ≥1 strong image per program (Children, Elderly, Women, Youth, Environment, Health).
- **Ratio:** 4:3 or 3:2 section; 1:1 for cards.
- **Candidates:** Children `2.jpg`,`bags.JPG`,`child 2.22.JPG`; Elderly `3.jpg`,`aged 2.22.JPG`; Women `1.jpg`,`tailor grop.JPG`; Youth `skills.JPG`,PPT`image29/30`; Environment `Save Rain Water.jpg`,PPT`image51/52/53`; Health — **weak** (extract from camp posters/PPT) **[Verify/source]**.
- **Existing sufficient?** Yes for 5; **Health needs sourcing**.
- **Extraction:** Yes (from posters/PPTs). **Adobe/Canva:** Yes (crop text, upscale).

### 3. Timeline images
- **Need:** small images for ~10–15 milestones.
- **Ratio:** 1:1 or 4:3 thumbnails.
- **Quality:** medium acceptable (small display).
- **Candidates:** era photos in PPT2 (`image21` Children's Club/Save the Children; `image29` Vardavalli), reports' embedded photos, program standalones.
- **Existing sufficient?** Partially; some milestones will use **theme icons** instead (never fabricate a photo).
- **Extraction:** Yes (reports/PPTs). **Adobe/Canva:** Yes (crop, restore). **Do NOT embed the infographic JPEGs.**

### 4. Story images
- **Need:** 3–6, consent-gated.
- **Ratio:** 4:5 portrait or 3:2.
- **Quality:** medium+.
- **Candidates:** consented program photos; **non-identifying** imagery by default; PPT1/WP portraits **only with consent [Verify]**.
- **Existing sufficient?** Only after **consent**; default to non-identifying.
- **Extraction/Editing:** case-by-case.

### 5. Gallery images
- **Need:** ~30–60 curated across categories.
- **Ratio:** mixed (masonry) with defined crops.
- **Quality:** medium; low-res allowed as small tiles only.
- **Candidates:** all poster/PPT standalone photos [Client]; current-site 33 gallery images [Web]; WP 2010 gallery (~30, low-res) [WP] — **de-duplicate across all three**.
- **Existing sufficient?** Yes in volume; **quality/curation** is the work.
- **Extraction:** Yes (many). **Adobe/Canva:** Yes (crop, upscale, standardize).

### 6. Press images
- **Need:** 4 clipping thumbnails + enlargements.
- **Ratio:** as-scanned; card thumb 4:3.
- **Candidates:** the 4 WhatsApp press clippings [Client] (best: Nava Telangana 31/12/2023, 1600×1001).
- **Existing sufficient?** For thumbnails yes; **request e-paper/higher-res** + **reuse permission [Verify]**.
- **Extraction/Editing:** light crop/clean.

### 7. Background / ambient images
- **Need:** a few section backgrounds/textures.
- **Candidates:** PPT2 `image1.jpeg` (2910×1974 "helping hands" — highest-res, but **likely stock → verify license [Verify]**); or brand-colored gradients/patterns derived from the logo (safer).
- **Existing sufficient?** Prefer **brand patterns/gradients** over risky stock.
- **Editing:** design-generated.

### 8. Decorative / brand assets
- **Need:** logo (header/footer/favicon), program icons, timeline branch motif, section dividers.
- **Candidates:** `SAHARANGOLOGO.png` (1254×1254; **obtain transparent/vector master [Verify]**); icon set to be designed; banyan-leaf motif from logo.
- **Editing:** logo background-removal/vectorize; custom icon/illustration set.

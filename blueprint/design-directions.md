# SAHARA — Design Directions (3 concepts)

**Phase 2 — direction only. No final design, no code.** Three visual directions to choose from, all anchored to SAHARA's identity: a **green banyan/spreading tree + black "SAHARA" wordmark** [Client logo], a 35+ year grassroots history, and beneficiaries spanning children, women, youth and elders.

Required character (all three): **human, trustworthy, warm, modern, professional, authentic, non-profit.** Avoid: corporate-startup gloss; childish/cartoonish (children are *among* beneficiaries, not the whole story); poverty clichés.

Color anchor for all: the logo's **tree green** + **near-black wordmark**; warm neutral paper background; one warm accent for CTAs. **[Verify]** exact brand hex from a clean logo master.

---

## Direction A — "Rooted & Warm" (recommended default)
- **Philosophy:** A grounded, human charity with deep roots — the banyan tree as a metaphor for shelter (*"sahara"* = support). Editorial warmth over flashiness.
- **Color:** Deep foliage **green** (primary) + charcoal text on **warm off-white/cream**; **terracotta/marigold** accent for CTAs (Indian-warm, high contrast). Soft green tints for section bands.
- **Typography:** Humanist serif for headlines (credible, timeless, editorial) + clean humanist sans for body (e.g. a serif/sans pairing — *[Verify]* final families for Latin **and Telugu** support if bilingual).
- **Image treatment:** Full-color, warm grade; gentle rounded corners; soft shadows; subtle duotone-green only for backgrounds.
- **Cards:** Soft-rounded, generous padding, thin border or low shadow, image-led.
- **Nav:** Transparent-over-hero → solid cream; underline-on-hover; prominent solid Donate button.
- **Buttons:** Solid terracotta primary; outline-green secondary; fully rounded or 8px radius.
- **Animation:** Restrained — fades, gentle rises on scroll; banyan-leaf/branch motifs on the timeline. Reduced-motion friendly.
- **Emotional character:** Warm, dignified, rooted, hopeful.
- **Why it fits:** Balances *heritage* (serif, roots motif) with *modern warmth*; the terracotta accent feels Indian and human without being childish; strongest for donor trust + emotion.

## Direction B — "Modern Grassroots" (clean, confident, contemporary)
- **Philosophy:** A modern NGO that's efficient and credible; content-forward, lots of whitespace, photography as the star.
- **Color:** Logo **green** + **white**, generous negative space; a single bright secondary (fresh leaf-green or warm amber) for CTAs; minimal tints.
- **Typography:** Contemporary geometric/neo-grotesque sans throughout; strong weight contrast for hierarchy (*[Verify]* Telugu support).
- **Image treatment:** Big, bright, full-bleed photography; crisp rectangular crops; minimal effects; consistent color grade to unify mixed-quality sources.
- **Cards:** Flat, crisp, thin dividers; grid-driven; image-top.
- **Nav:** Solid white, sticky, minimal; pill Donate button.
- **Buttons:** Solid green primary; ghost secondary; small radius.
- **Animation:** Subtle micro-interactions; number count-ups; smooth section reveals.
- **Emotional character:** Clean, credible, optimistic, "well-run."
- **Why it fits:** Reads as a capable modern organization; great for funders/CSR/verifiers; relies on strong photography (needs the best enhanced assets).

## Direction C — "Heritage & Dignity" (editorial, story-led, timeless)
- **Philosophy:** Honor a long history; a documentary/editorial feel that foregrounds people and journey. Storytelling-first.
- **Color:** Muted deep green + **ink black** + **archival cream/paper**; restrained, slightly desaturated; small brass/gold accent for milestones/awards.
- **Typography:** Elegant serif display + serif/sans body; larger type, longform-friendly measure; strong editorial rhythm.
- **Image treatment:** Mixed full-color and tasteful **duotone** for historical/archive images (unifies low-res material and signals "history"); framed captions.
- **Cards:** Editorial blocks, hairline rules, caption-led; magazine layout.
- **Nav:** Serif wordmark lockup; slim top bar; understated Donate.
- **Buttons:** Refined, medium radius; green/ink; gold hover for key CTAs.
- **Animation:** Minimal, dignified; timeline as the signature interactive piece.
- **Emotional character:** Timeless, respectful, credible, human.
- **Why it fits:** Turns the **history/timeline/reports** (SAHARA's strongest assets) into the design centerpiece; the duotone trick gracefully handles low-res archival photos. Slightly less "donate-punchy" than A.

---

## Recommendation
**Direction A ("Rooted & Warm")** as the primary, borrowing **B's clean photo grid** for programs/gallery and **C's duotone treatment** specifically for the **historical archive/timeline**. This yields warmth + credibility + a graceful way to handle mixed-quality historical imagery.

## Cross-direction system (to define at design phase, not now)
- **Color tokens** from the verified logo (green scale, ink, cream, one warm accent) with **AA+ contrast** (see `accessibility.md`).
- **Type scale** (fluid, mobile-first) + **Telugu font** if bilingual is chosen **[Verify]**.
- **Components:** buttons, cards, badges (80G/12A/FCRA — shown only when verified), counters, timeline node, gallery tile, report card, press card.
- **Iconography:** custom simple line icons per program; banyan-leaf motif.
- **Motion tokens:** durations/easings; all gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

> These are directions to choose from, not the final design. No visual asset is produced or edited in this phase.

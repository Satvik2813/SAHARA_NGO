# SAHARA

## Extending a Helping Hand. Creating a Better Tomorrow.

For decades, Sahara has been working to support poor, underprivileged and vulnerable communities by creating opportunities for education, empowerment, healthcare and sustainable development.

Established in 1986 and registered in 1988, Sahara works across communities in Telangana with a simple belief — **every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope and independence.**

## Our Mission

To serve poor and needy communities, especially youth in rural areas and urban slums, and help them build a better and more secure future.

We work to empower women, support the elderly, improve education for disadvantaged children, promote health and hygiene, strengthen communities and protect our environment.

## Our Vision

A society where every child can learn, every woman can become independent, every elderly person can live with dignity, and every community has the opportunity to grow sustainably.

## What We Do

### 🎓 Children & Education

Every child deserves a chance to learn and dream.

Sahara supports children from disadvantaged backgrounds, including orphan, semi-orphan and poor girl children, helping them continue their education through educational materials, nutrition, clothing and financial support.

Through joyful learning centres, child labour special schools and child-rights initiatives, Sahara works to bring children back into education and create better opportunities for their future.

### 👵 Care for the Elderly

No elderly person should feel forgotten.

Sahara supports destitute and uncared-for elderly people with essential provisions, nutrition, toiletries, healthcare and regular assistance. Awareness programmes are also conducted to encourage communities and younger generations to care for senior citizens.

### 👩 Women Empowerment

Empowered women build stronger families and stronger communities.

Sahara works with women from vulnerable communities through Self Help Groups, vocational training, financial literacy, self-employment initiatives, career counselling and livelihood programmes.

Training in areas such as tailoring, beautician services, computer skills, workplace English and office assistance helps women develop practical skills and create opportunities for sustainable income.

### 💼 Youth Skill Development

Skills can change the direction of a life.

Sahara provides vocational and employability training for unemployed youth, school and college dropouts and young women. Training includes computer basics, workplace English, office assistance, tailoring, driving, hardware, lab-related skills and other employment-oriented programmes.

Training is complemented by placement assistance and career counselling to help participants move towards employment and financial independence.

### 🌱 Environment & Sustainability

Protecting nature means protecting our future.

Through tree plantation, rainwater harvesting, water recharge, biodiversity awareness, climate-change campaigns, waste management and other environmental initiatives, Sahara encourages communities to take responsibility for the environment.

Under its “Grow More Trees” campaign, more than a lakh saplings have been planted and supported over the years, along with the construction of rainwater recycling and recharge units.

### 🏥 Health & Hygiene

Better health creates stronger communities.

Sahara conducts health awareness programmes and medical camps for children, women and elderly people, with a focus on communities that have limited access to healthcare.

Its initiatives include awareness on HIV/AIDS, cancer, diabetic retinopathy, hygiene and seasonal diseases, along with regular health and dental camps.

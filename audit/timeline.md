## Year-by-year (from the 30-Year Journey infographics)

### 1990 — Cyclone relief
- **ACTIVITY:** Disaster relief.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Collected old clothes and essential commodities and distributed them to cyclone-affected victims of Krishna and East Godavari districts. More than **350** victims benefited. (Horizontal timeline phrases this as Sahara "began its journey" with cyclone relief.)
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.17 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High (client graphic) · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Exact victim count.

### 1992 — Free eye camps for leprosy
- **ACTIVITY:** Health camps.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Supported the Government Leprosy Control Unit of Medak district in organizing free eye camps exclusively for leprosy patients. More than **1,200** patients benefited.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.17 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Patient count.

### 1996 — Formation of Self Help Groups
- **ACTIVITY:** Women / SHGs.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Encouraged women to establish self-help groups and save money for self-reliance. **Eight villages of Boinpalli Mandal** formed SHGs and began thrift & credit activities with their savings.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.17)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Village/mandal spelling.

### 1998 — Sewing centre
- **ACTIVITY:** Women / vocational training.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Started a sewing centre at Vilasagar village, Karimnagar district; over **75** women received tailoring training. A sewing centre for child labour was also established at CLPS school, Koresm.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.17)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Place names ("Vilasagar", "Koresm").

### 2001 — Prevention of blindness among children
- **ACTIVITY:** Health / children.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Awareness programmes to prevent blindness caused by Vitamin A deficiency; Vitamin A capsules distributed to **3,500** children under 6 years old.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.17)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count.

### 2003 — Beedi-rolling women project
- **ACTIVITY:** Women / livelihoods.
- **DESCRIPTION:** A project for socio-economic development of beedi-rolling women through capacity building, implemented in **10 villages of Tadwai Mandal, Nizamabad district**, covering more than **2,000** women beedi workers.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.17 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count. (PPT2 also cites 600 beedi-rolling *girl children* mainstreamed — related but distinct figure.)

### 2008 — Adoption of elders
- **ACTIVITY:** Elderly care.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Adopted **94** elderly individuals in Karimnagar district and Hyderabad slums, providing regular monthly support in kind and cash to the most deserving and destitute elderly throughout the year.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.17)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count.

### 2009 — Rainwater recharge & recycling model units
- **ACTIVITY:** Environment / water.
- **DESCRIPTION:** **19** rainwater recycling model units constructed in various villages; awareness created among students, parents, teachers, PRI members and youth on saving rainwater.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.18)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count (cf. cumulative "250+ units" in writeup).

### 2010 — "Grow More Trees"
- **ACTIVITY:** Environment / plantation.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Tree plantation under the "Grow More Trees" campaign; approximately **10,000** saplings planted in various school compounds, with the assistance of students, youth and elderly individuals.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.18 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count (cf. cumulative "1 Lakh+ saplings" in writeup).

### 2011 — Health care for the aged & children
- **ACTIVITY:** Health camps.
- **DESCRIPTION:** **9** health camps organized in Amberpet slum areas, benefiting approximately **907** elderly and children; regular monthly health check-ups for sponsored children and adopted aged.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.18)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Counts.

### 2013 — Youth & women training programmes
- **ACTIVITY:** Skills / employability.
- **DESCRIPTION:** Beauty-culture training for **28** young women / school-dropout girls; basic computer training with spoken English and life skills; **37** candidates trained and placed in better jobs for the first time; more than **300** candidates received counselling during the year.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.18 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Counts.

### 2015 — Solar lights promotion
- **ACTIVITY:** Environment / energy awareness.
- **DESCRIPTION:** To promote solar lamps/lanterns and home systems in rural and urban areas, **Sri Ben Mathew** gave a demo to women's group members explaining the utility of solar lights and power savings.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.18)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Name "Sri Ben Mathew".

### 2018 — Kerala flood relief
- **ACTIVITY:** Disaster relief.
- **DESCRIPTION:** SAHARA provided assistance to Kerala flood victims; relief activities carried out by senior staff member **Ms. Sajitha**, in collaboration with local volunteers and SHG members.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographics (.18 and .20)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High. (Consistent with staff "N. Sajitha, Project Coordinator".)

### 2020 — COVID-19 relief
- **ACTIVITY:** Disaster / health relief.
- **DESCRIPTION:** From **March 2020**, SAHARA provided support and relief to vulnerable families — distributing ration kits, essential items, sanitizers and masks — and educated people about safe health and hygiene practices to prevent the spread of COVID-19.
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.20, horizontal). (PPT1 also references post-COVID funding struggles.)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Appears only on the horizontal timeline (not the vertical set).

### 2021 — Menstrual health & hygiene
- **ACTIVITY:** Health / girls.
- **DESCRIPTION:** As part of promoting health-seeking behaviour and menstrual-hygiene awareness among young girls, sanitary napkins were regularly distributed to more than **30** girls and young women from weaker-section families, with donor support — "Bleed without Barriers".
- **SOURCE:** Timeline infographic (.18)
- **CONFIDENCE:** High · **NEEDS VERIFICATION:** Count.

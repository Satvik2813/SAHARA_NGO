## 6. Core program areas / beneficiary groups

Six core areas are consistent across the writeup, both PPTs and the website:

### 6.1 Children & Education **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- Support for disadvantaged children — orphan, semi-orphan, poor girl children, single-parent children.
- Educational materials, tuition fees, nutrition, clothing, toiletries, healthcare, emergency needs.
- Joyful learning centres, child-labour special schools, child-rights initiatives, children's clubs.
- Support for higher education (intermediate, graduation, post-graduation).
- Notable model: **"Paying It Back"** — former beneficiaries (girls supported ~10 years earlier) now donate to support current children. **[CLIENT PPT1]**

### 6.2 Care for the Elderly / Aged **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- "Adopt a Destitute Aged" / "Adopt a Granny" — lifetime support for uncared-for elderly: monthly provisions, nutrition, toiletries, healthcare.
- Awareness campaigns among younger generations (school painting/essay competitions).
- Health camps for the elderly (Amberpet slums; Suraksha Centre, R.C. Puram / BHEL).

### 6.3 Women Empowerment **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- Self Help Groups (SHGs), thrift & credit, financial literacy.
- Vocational training: tailoring, fashion design, beautician/beauty culture, computer skills, workplace/spoken English, office assistant.
- Career counselling, self-employment and livelihood programmes.

### 6.4 Youth Skill Development **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- Vocational & employability training for unemployed youth and school/college dropouts.
- Computer basics, workplace English, office assistance, tailoring, driving, hardware, lab-related skills.
- Life-skills training, placement assistance, career counselling.

### 6.5 Environment & Sustainability **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- **"Grow More Trees"** campaign (tree plantation).
- Rainwater harvesting / recycling / recharge units.
- Biodiversity awareness, climate-change campaigns, waste management, reducing plastic usage, vermicomposting, sustainable agriculture.

### 6.6 Health & Hygiene **[CLIENT][WEB]**
- Health awareness programmes and medical/dental camps for children, women, elderly.
- Awareness on HIV/AIDS, cancer, diabetic retinopathy, hygiene, seasonal diseases.
- Menstrual health & hygiene ("Bleed without Barriers"; sanitary napkin distribution).
